## How to use in PRDs

1. **Research:** When searching Slack, include competitor-specific searches: `"Cresta" in:<relevant_channel>`, `"Sierra" "SRA"`, `"Google CCAI"`, etc. The SE Collaboration (`C08E300HPUK`) and FDE Collaboration (`C0AN1E181M3`) channels frequently surface competitive intel from customer conversations.

2. **Business Case / Why This Matters:** Reference specific competitive threats when they strengthen the urgency argument. E.g., "EA is in active talks with Sierra" or "Cresta was the primary competitor in UPS's POC."

3. **Relevant Research Insights (Full PRD):** Populate the competitor table with feature-level comparisons relevant to the specific PRD. Don't list all competitors generically — only include those whose capabilities directly overlap with the feature being proposed.

4. **One-pager (Why This Matters):** Fold competitive positioning into a single principle if it strengthens urgency. E.g., "Cresta and Sierra both offer workflow enforcement as core capabilities — without [feature], we lose enterprise credibility."

5. **Differentiation framing:** Always frame SRA's advantage as:
   - **Console-native** (not a separate screen/tool)
   - **CRM-grounded** (uses actual case, account, contact data — not just conversation context)
   - **Admin-configurable** (Topics, Actions, Policies, Eligibility Flows — not black-box AI)
   - **Multi-channel** (Case + Messaging + Voice in one framework)
   - **Human-in-the-loop** (augments reps for complex/regulated workflows where autonomy isn't appropriate)
